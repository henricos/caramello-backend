## Overview

Complete implementation of the financial domain: bank accounts scoped by family, movements with batch import and automatic deduplication, two-level hierarchical categories, semi-automatic reconciliation with similarity-based suggestion, and analytical reports of balance and breakdown by competência.

Beyond the business domain, the milestone delivered structural improvements to the DSL: support for `Decimal→NUMERIC(15,2)`, `filters:→Index`, the `expose_as_uuid` flag for protecting internal IDs, and formal synchronization of the business operations in the DSL together with reinforcement of the DSL-first rule.

## Milestone Summary

### Delivered (25 business endpoints)

- Account, Category, Subcategory CRUD with family-scoped access
- Individual recording of a movement (credit/debit)
- Batch CSV/OFX/XLSX import with SHA-256 deduplication
- Movement→FinancialEntry reconciliation (1:1, 409 if duplicated)
- Suggest category via `rapidfuzz.token_set_ratio`
- Balance per account and consolidated family balance
- Monthly breakdown by parent category/subcategory and by member

### Structural improvements (DSL + generator)

- `expose_as_uuid: true` — int FKs never leak into public schemas
- `filters:` → `Index` in `__table_args__` declared in the YAML
- finances DSL operations synchronized retroactively (25 ops)
- `dsl_rules.md` expanded with Part II — rules for operations

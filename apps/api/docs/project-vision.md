# Caramello Project Vision

## 1. Product Purpose

Caramello is a personal, integrated system for family organization, created to centralize, simplify and make collaborative the management of the family's day-to-day life. Its purpose is to reduce information fragmentation, lower the cognitive effort required to organize domestic life and offer a shared view — while respecting individuality and privacy.

Caramello does not seek to replace established market services, but to act as a central point of entry, organization and interaction for the family.

---

## 2. Target Audience

Caramello is aimed primarily at:

* Families that share domestic and organizational responsibilities
* Couples (with or without children)
* Families with school-age children
* Families that care for relatives (elderly, dependents, etc.)

The system takes into account that, in many families, one or more people are informally responsible for "organizing everything". Caramello seeks to distribute that load better among the members, promoting collaboration and transparency.

---

## 3. Problems Caramello Solves

Caramello is born to tackle common problems of everyday family life, such as:

* Information scattered across multiple applications and platforms
* Lack of a unified view of the family's calendar and responsibilities
* Difficulty keeping shared lists up to date
* Loss of important information (medical exams, appointments, relevant dates)
* Excessive dependence on a single person as the family's "organizer"

---

## 4. Product Principles

The principles below guide decisions on scope, UX and evolution of the system:

* **Conscious centralization**: gather information in a single place without duplicating already established services
* **Family collaboration**: allow everyone to contribute and follow what is relevant
* **Separation between the individual and the collective**: not everything is shared, and that must be respected
* **Simplicity**: reduce friction and complexity in daily use
* **Trust and privacy**: handle sensitive data with care and explicit control
* **Incremental evolution**: start with a solid core and expand gradually

---

## 5. Conceptual Usage Model

Caramello organizes the experience around the concept of the **family** as the central nucleus. Within that nucleus:

* There is **shared** information, visible to all members
* There is **individual** information, associated with a specific member
* There is **sensitive** information, whose access may be restricted

Collaboration is driven by context: calendar, lists, tasks, health, entertainment, etc.

---

## 6. Functional Scope (High Level)

Over the course of its evolution, Caramello intends to offer functionality grouped into the domains below.
**Note:** This list is illustrative and not exhaustive. Specific features will be prioritized, detailed and refined gradually through PRDs and future validations.

### 6.1 Organization and Planning

* Family and individual calendar
* Appointments, events and reminders
* Visualization by member or by family

### 6.2 Lists and Collaboration

* Collaborative shopping list
* Wish list for gifts
* Individual and shared task lists

### 6.3 Health and Well-Being

* Repository of medical exams and documents
* History of consultations
* Medication reminders
* Record of menstrual cycles and mood variations

### 6.4 Entertainment and Preferences

* Lists of movies and series of interest
* Book lists
* Record of favorite songs (individual use)

### 6.5 Family Information

* Calendar of birthdays and important dates
* Base of relevant family information

### 6.6 Family Financial Management

* Definition of monthly budget and income
* Bank integration to track spending (reading of statement/card)
* Monitoring of expenses by category with charts and alerts
* View of the forecast budget "overrun"

---

## 7. Out of Scope (Non-Goals)

Caramello does **not** aim to:

* Replace official medical record systems
* Act as Internet Banking (making payments or transfers)
* Be a social network
* Compete directly with services such as Google Calendar, Spotify or streaming platforms
* Become a corporate or professional system

---

## 8. Evolution Vision

Caramello's development is conceived in waves:

### Initial Core

* Registration of users and families
* Member management
* Family calendar
* Basic collaborative lists

### Natural Expansions

* Family health
* Entertainment
* Information and documents

### Future Evolutions

* Natural language interaction
* Virtual assistant
* Automation of family routines

This evolution vision allows continuous growth without compromising the clarity and stability of the product's core.

---

## 9. Final Considerations

The Caramello Project Vision serves as the conceptual and functional reference for all future product decisions. This document does not describe technical, architectural or implementation details, which are handled separately.

Its goal is to ensure alignment, consistency and clarity throughout the evolution of the system.
